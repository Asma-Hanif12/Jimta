# 🛍️ Jimta

**Jimta** is a complete eCommerce platform that combines online shopping, service booking, digital wallet, rewards, and content management into one ecosystem. It includes a customer website, mobile application, and a powerful admin panel for managing products, users, and business operations.

## ✨ Features

### 🛒 Shopping
- Browse Products
- Product Categories
- Shopping Cart
- Easy Checkout

### 🛠️ Services
- Service Listings
- Service Booking
- Business Offerings

### 💳 Wallet & Rewards
- Digital Wallet
- Jimta Coins Reward System
- Wallet Balance Management
- Reward Redemption

### 📝 Content
- Blogs
- News & Updates
- Promotional Content

### 📱 Customer Platform
- Website
- Mobile Application
- User Accounts
- Order Management

### 🛠️ Admin Panel
Manage the entire platform from a centralized dashboard:

- 👥 User Management
- 📦 Product Management
- 🛒 Order Management
- 🛠️ Service Management
- 💳 Wallet & Jimta Coins
- 📝 Blog Management
- 📊 Reports & Analytics

## 🚀 Why Jimta?

Jimta delivers a complete digital commerce experience by combining shopping, services, rewards, and content into one platform. Customers enjoy a seamless purchasing experience, while administrators efficiently manage products, services, users, and business operations.
